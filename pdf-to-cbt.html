📄 PDF to CBT Generator

Upload a question paper PDF, optionally upload its answer-key PDF, review every extracted question, correct mistakes, then generate a standalone CBT HTML file. English-left/Hindi-right two-column papers and letter or numbered options are supported with best-effort layout detection.

Important: This tool prioritizes the left/English column on two-column pages. Diagrams are embedded as page snapshots. Verify every question, option and answer before publishing.
